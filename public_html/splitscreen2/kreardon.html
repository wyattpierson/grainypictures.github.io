Kerry Reardon
At the present, videographer Kerry Reardon tries to tap the viewer on the shoulder with her 10, 20 and 40 second video shorts, which she presents as modern day allegories. "Seconds, please" can be, at times, a bit mundane to say the least, but her approach in introducing the viewer emotionally sometimes by sound alone encourages introspection even before her visual imagery arrives. Her work will be featured amongst a core of participating videomakers, musicians and documentarians in the premiere issue of an on-line magazine called "N9" coming out in February 2000.
