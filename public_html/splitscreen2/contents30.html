Episode #30 - Season Three

For the final episode of our '98 season, Split Screen will, for the first time in its history, devote the entire show to a single subject - regional filmmaker par excellence, Jacob Young, in his own backyard. Come meet the artist who is equally at home with an Elvis channeling schizoprenic tap dancer, cross planting evangelical, cranky chemical plant owner and a pedophile Swami. Directed by Split Screen all-star Amy Elliot, we proudly present the filmmaker who to us, embodies the true spirit of independent film.
