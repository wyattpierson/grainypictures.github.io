Split Screen 1999

You can forget that wild ride stuff.

Welcome to the all-new 1999 Split Screen season. In order to be first in line when Star Wars opens, we're camping out in front of NYC's Ziegfeld Theatre - home of the original film in 1977. Sure it's three months early. But once we heard the fanboys would be queuing up by late April, we figured why take any chances?

But have no fear. While we're saving gas money, our relentless band of filmmakers are traveling coast to coast, searching high and low for the best stories in independent film.
