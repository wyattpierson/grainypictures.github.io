Split Screen #19 premieres on The Independent Film Channel Monday, June 1st at 8:00pm, EST/8:00pm, PST

SPLIT SCREEN, the irreverent, sly magazine format show which takes an inside look at the tremendous diversity of filmmaking in the United States returns for another season. Join John Pierson and the Split Screen crew as they travel around the country visiting noted filmmakers and talented unknowns.
