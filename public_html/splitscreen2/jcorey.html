Some of the talented filmmakers who work on Split Screen:

Joe Corey
Joe Corey III is wrapping up a film degree at the North Caroline School of the Arts in Winston-Salem. He programs NCSA's three theaters; choosing between the 20,000 35mm prints in the school's collection.

"A/V Geeks" marks Joe's third appearance on Split Screen. A hockey puck shown off in the pilot episode was from him. Joe produced and wwrote "Man and Dog" with director Randy Benson (epsiode 23). "A/V Geeks" marks Joe's directorial debut.

Joe produced Chad Nance's "Outer Banks." He writes the Party Favors column for www.tibbysbowl.com. He has a website at www.geocities.com/hollywood/hills/9956.
