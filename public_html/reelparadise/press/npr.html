'Reel Paradise': Moving Theater Experience in Fiji

by Alex Chadwick

Day to Day, September 20, 2005 á American movie buff and independent filmmaker John Pierson moved his family to Fiji in 2002 in search of "the world's most remote theater." The documentary Reel Paradise chronicles his experience showing free films to Fijians, and the surprising reactions he got to some of his movie choices. Alex Chadwick speaks with the documentary's director Steve James.
