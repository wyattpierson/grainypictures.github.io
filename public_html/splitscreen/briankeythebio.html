Bio - Keythe Farley and Brian Femming

Brian Flemming and Keythe Farley are longtime collaborators in theater, film, television and music. A recent collaboration is the feature film Hang Your Dog in the Wind, which Brian wrote and directed and in which Keythe starred. The film, currently on a festival tour, has played to critical acclaim in festivals in Park City, Utah; New York; Chicago; Florida and Germany. Hang Your Dog in the Wind won a Special Jury Prize at the 1997 Florida Film Festival.

Brian and Keythe also co-wrote the book for BAT BOY: THE MUSICAL, an extravagant stage production about Bat Boy, a half-boy/half-bat popularized in the Weekly World News, a supermarket tabloid. The production premiered at the Actors' Gang Theater in Los Angeles, CA in November of 1997.

Keythe, Brian and others also founded The Slumdance Experience, a film festival, in Park City, Utah in January 1997. Slumdance was a renegade alternative to the other, more sober film festivals that run at the same time in Park City: Sundance and Slamdance. Slumdance maintains a website at www.slumdance.com.
