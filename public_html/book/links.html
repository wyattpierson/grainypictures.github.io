The following links are not only helpful and informative resources, they are also near and dear to our heart.

Great Sites

Filmmaker Magazine - One of the best Filmmaking publications available.

View Askew Productions - Home of former 'clerk' and present filmmaker, writer, and director Kevin Smith.

The Independent Film Channel - home of Split Screen.

Bravo - IFC's sister station and home of "IFC Friday nights" featuring Split Screen in Spring '98.

Indiewire - Independent film news delivered to you daily.

AIVF - filmmaker support organization.

Sundance channel website - Forget the channel itself which can barely be seen and (treated us shabbily), but we like the website. It covers the world of indie film with some terrific writing.


Links to our Split Screen family sites
Slumdance - a hilarious site and brilliant concept created by our Split Screen regulars (#1, #5, #7) Brian Flemming with Keythe Farley.

Swingblade - featured in Split Screen #7. Here's the director's homepage.

Fueltour - Four filmmakers taking it to the people - one of whom is Chris Smith (both on air and behind the camera in Split Screen #3, #4, #6, #7, #8) with American Job, one of our favorite films of the last several years.

SubUrbia - featured in Split Screen #2.

Home of the Florida Film Festival (Split Screen #7) - and a movie theatre where you can eat and drink while you're watching.
