The people / Janet

Janet
Pierson.

Championing filmmakers.
Making room for the unexpected.

A career in independent film

Exhibitor. Producer’s representative. Executive producer. Programmer. A connecting thread: helping filmmakers reach people.

A champion
behind the scenes.

Janet’s work spans exhibition, representation, producing, and programming. At Grainy Pictures, that included executive producing and directing segments for Split Screen, as well as the family’s year running a cinema in Fiji, documented in Steve James’s Reel Paradise.

She also appeared in Andrew Bujalski’s Beeswax and curated In the Dark: Filmmakers Illuminate, Stories from the Film World for The Moth in 2001.

The SXSW years.

Beginning in 2008, Janet led SXSW Film through a period that embraced new filmmakers, television, and immersive work. In October 2022, she moved into a Director Emeritus role, continuing on the programming team for the 2023 festival.

  1. 2008

    A new chapter in Austin

    Janet takes the helm of SXSW Film, with her first festival following in 2009.

  2. 2014

    Room for episodic storytelling

    SXSW launches a dedicated Episodic screening section, expanding the festival’s canvas.

  3. 2022

    Passing the baton

    Janet transitions to Director Emeritus; Claudette Godfrey assumes leadership.

  4. 2023

    The Janet Pierson Champion Award

    SXSW establishes an award in her honor, recognizing the work of championing independent filmmakers.
