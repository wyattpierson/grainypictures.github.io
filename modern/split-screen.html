Beyond the usual
making-of.

Part movie magazine, part road trip, part encounter with the unexpected. Split Screen brought independent filmmakers and their worlds to television in a half-hour magazine format.

John created and hosted the series; Janet worked as an executive producer and segment director. Across its four-year run, the show put more than 100 independent filmmakers to work.

Its stories intersected with films including The Blair Witch Project and How’s Your News? The original site holds the episode guides, credits, and filmmaker pages that grew around the show.
