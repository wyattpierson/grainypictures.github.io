The people / John

John
Pierson.

Connecting independent filmmakers with the audiences their movies deserve.

A life between
films and audiences.

As a producer’s representative and supporter of first-time filmmakers, John played a part in bringing work by Spike Lee, Michael Moore, Richard Linklater, and Kevin Smith to the screen. Those experiences became the basis of his book Spike, Mike, Slackers & Dykes, later revised as Spike, Mike Reloaded.

He created and hosted Split Screen for IFC, and executive produced Chasing Amy. With Janet, his work has included film festivals, workshops, and completion funding.

A detour to Fiji

The Pierson family spent a year showing free movies at the 180 Meridian Cinema on Taveuni. Their adventure became the subject of Steve James’s documentary Reel Paradise. The family’s dispatches and photographs remain in the original site.
