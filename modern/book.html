Spike, Mike
Reloaded.

A guided tour across a decade of American independent cinema. By John Pierson.

The 2004 edition

From the inside
of the indie explosion.

Before these filmmakers became familiar names, there were first features, unfinished films, and distribution deals to figure out. John Pierson was there, helping bring work by Spike Lee, Michael Moore, Richard Linklater, and Kevin Smith to audiences.

Originally published as Spike, Mike, Slackers & Dykes, and revised and reissued in 2004 as Spike, Mike Reloaded, the book tells that story from inside the business of independent film.

The Reloaded edition includes a new foreword and afterword by Kevin Smith. For another edition of the original title, visit the University of Texas Press.
