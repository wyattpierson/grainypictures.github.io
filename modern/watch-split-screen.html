Reading the guide

Use the introductions as the starting point for each Criterion season. Episode labels follow the titles on Criterion, so an introduction never counts as Episode 1 here.

Season Two’s Wes Anderson interview is listed after Episode 5 on Criterion, without a season/episode label in its title. We place it sixth. Season Nine’s Kevin Smith entry links to both original episodes, #49 and #50.

Segment names are a compact index from our original guide. Open “Episode details” or “Credits” for the full historical record.
